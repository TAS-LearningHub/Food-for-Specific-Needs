# Food for Specific Needs - build scope v1.0

## Included

- Connected course landing page and five modules covering 15 named theory sections.
- Substantial theory, knowledge checks with feedback, guided written responses and browser-local saving.
- Fifteen purposeful teaching diagrams with captions, alt text and full-size routes.
- Five classroom-ready eight-slide PowerPoint decks.
- Section-specific video-learning library with watch-for prompts and click-to-load privacy-enhanced embeds where validated.
- Course-specific Puzzles / Busy Work activities with feedback and saved evidence.
- Student folio with staged evidence, autosave, backup, restore, reset protection, print and submission export.
- Source-backed recipe library using the nine teacher-posted recipe masters.
- Current 2026 Year 9 assessment schedule, with the old 2024 notice quarantined as historical and no invented current notification.
- Teacher Resources containing the source map, editable program, delivery ledger, manifests and Teacher-to-confirm register.

## Excluded

- Student names, submissions, grades, photographs, email addresses and student-copy URLs.
- Answer keys and assessor-only records.
- Unconfirmed medical advice, local allergy-management procedures, recipe substitutions, practical competence claims and current task dates.
- Stage 13 section screenshot codes; they were not requested and add no essential student-learning value to this build.
- Any commit, push, deployment, Main Page edit or Classroom publication.

## Required routes

`index.html`, five `modules/module-0N.html` routes, `visual.html`, `videos.html`, `puzzles.html`, `activities/index.html`, `folio.html`, `recipes.html`, `assessment.html`, `teacher-resources.html`, and downloadable presentation/program resources.

## Operational states

- Source and scope: mapped.
- Publication boundary: local only.
- Completion target: frozen local candidate, content/visual audits and consolidated course acceptance.
